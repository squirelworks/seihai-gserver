# Lab run: the NPC-server link (2026-10-31)

**What:** `seihai-gserver` (this branch) with `npcserver = auto`, and the real
`seihai-npcserver` binary (main at `340bfa7`) copied next to it, in a Linux
container on `127.0.0.1`. The sample world with one level file and the example
NPC from `seihai-npcserver/examples/world`.

**Before:** `npclink.rs` was a skeleton; an NPC-server had nothing to connect to.

- The token never shows in either log or on a command line (it goes in the
  environment).
- Killing the NPC-server by hand: the gserver logs `exited`, starts it again
  1 s later, and it reconnects.
- Stopping the gserver (Ctrl-C) leaves no `seihai-npcserver` running.

**Players on the link** are covered by `tests/npc_link.rs`, with real logins
through `seihai_core::net`: a 2.22 player online before the NPC-server connects
arrives in the snapshot with all 19 own props; a 2.05 player who joins later is
sent live, its `PLI_PLAYERPROPS` arrive as `PLAYER_PROPS`, and its disconnect as
`PLAYER_LEFT`. A wrong token gets `REJECT "bad token"`; a second NPC-server gets
`REJECT "already connected"`. No real Windows clients were run for this change:
it does not change what clients see.
