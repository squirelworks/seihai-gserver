# Real 2.05 rev 2 with the NPC-server, and a board-change capture try, 2026-11-13

Loopback only. Everything ran in a Debian lab VM, inside a private network
namespace whose only interface is `lo`, with the clients under Wine
(`tools/lab-wine.md`). No public server and no real player account were used.

## Setup

- This server at `main` (`47d0cd5`), `cargo build --release`, run with
  `--verbose`. Its `npcserver = auto` started seihai-npcserver `main`
  (`d715451`, 0.1.2) built next to it.
- The lab listserver (seihai-listserver `ae213fc`), over the encrypted link.
- A logging relay in front of the gserver (127.0.0.1 to 127.0.0.2). It decodes
  zlib and gen-5 frames.
- The world was the 2026-11-12 NPC lab world, with two server-side NPCs on
  `onlinestartlocal.nw`:
  - `hello.txt`, a block at 30,30 that says "hello from the NPC-server";
  - `mover.txt`, `bomb1.png` from 26,26: every second x + 1 (back to 26 after
    36) and the message `tick` or `tock`, with "hi #a" on `playerenters`.

## #53: real 2.05 rev 2 sees the NPCs move and talk: pass

- 2.05 rev 2 (`GNW03081`, gen 3 on the wire) logged in through the listserver
  as player 1.
- It got both NPCs as `PLO_NPCPROPS` (3): `block.png` with "hello from the
  NPC-server", then `bomb1.png` with `tock`, `hi x205` and `tick`.
- Eight screenshots one second apart show the bomb NPC one tile further right
  each time, then back at the start. Its message alternated `tick` / `tock`
  every second while the block's message stayed.
- About 75 s after login it was still moving and switching messages. No timer
  warnings and no freeze: the stall seen in the Windows 10 lab VM on 2026-11-12
  did not happen under Wine.

Real 2.22 passed the same check on 2026-11-12, so both clients #53 named have
now seen the NPC appear, move and talk.

## #107: what a real client sends for a board change: not captured

The capture was tried with a real 2.22 (gen 5). The test level had a bush
(tiles 2, 3 / 18, 19) and, meant as a push stone, tiles 1764, 1765 / 1780, 1781.
Those tile numbers were wrong: the client drew a red icon there, not a stone.
On this server, `S` swung the sword (prop 10 `*%sword`, then file checks for
`sword.gani` and `sword.wav`), but no `PLI_BOARDMODIFY` (1) came in. The player
then walked through the bush tiles. On the fork's `default` world, the client's
attack key sent only `PLI_SHOWIMGPLAYER` (24); that world's `-gr_movement`
weapon seems to take the key. Digging was not tried. A rerun needs a level
whose stone and bush tiles are taken from a real level, not worked out by hand.
