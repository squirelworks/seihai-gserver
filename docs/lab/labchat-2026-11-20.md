# The lab chat weapon on real 5.24r4 and 6.03r7, 2026-11-20 (issue #174)

Real, unchanged Windows clients (5.24r4, 6.03r7 and 2.22) in the Windows 10
lab VM on Mirae, against this server at ced287d with `--verbose`. Loopback
only, guest logins, as in `stage6-win10-2026-11-18.md`. `-LabChat` from
`tools/lab-world` was given to the guest account only.

## Input line

- **Before (main, 2026-11-19 run):** the `> ` line never drew on 5.24r4 or
  6.03r7, though `onKeyPressed` ran and typed lines were sent as chat.
- **At ced287d (`changeimgvis 200,4` after `showtext`):** `> ` drew at the
  bottom left on both right after login, typed text showed in it, and
  Backspace removed one character.
- Three weapon defects showed up, fixed after this run:
  - A typed space was lost: "two words here" was sent as `twowordshere`.
    The bytecode joins `params[1]` onto the line with nothing trimmed, so
    the trailing space goes on the client. A space is now held and
    written with the next character.
  - Letters also acted as game keys: typing "words" dropped a bomb on
    6.03r7. The line now opens on Enter, with `disableweapons` until it
    closes.
  - Escape on 5.24r4 opened the client's own "Do you really want to quit
    Graal?" box, which then took no mouse clicks. The weapon no longer
    uses Escape.
- The open/close, held space and `disableweapons` change has not been
  run on a real client yet.

## Chat drawn as a narrow column

Not reproduced. Every line drew as one line on every receiver (5.24r4,
6.03r7, 2.22) and on the sender. `PLO_OTHERPLPROPS` (8) as the receivers
got it: id, a GSHORT player id, prop 12, a GCHAR length, the text.

| Sender | Text | Bytes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.22 | `hi222` | `28 20 25 2c 25 68 69 32 32 32` |
| 6.03r7 | `hi603` | `28 20 24 2c 25 68 69 36 30 33` |
| 6.03r7 | `twowordshere` | `28 20 24 2c 2c 74 77 6f 77 6f 72 64 73 68 65 72 65` |

The same layout as the 2026-11-19 capture, so the server's props were
not the cause; this run had no column on any receiver.

3000000 through prop 83. As designed.
