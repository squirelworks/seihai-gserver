# Player hits between real clients, 2026-11-12 (issue #50)

Real 2.05 rev 2 and 2.22 clients hurt and killed each other on
seihai-gserver with PR #80. On `main` before it, neither could hurt the
other. The run was loopback only, under Wine (`tools/lab-wine.md`). No
public server and no real player account were used.

## Setup

- Server: PR #80 (`claude/gserver-205-gani`, 6ba0dd2) and `main` (3c26e78),
  each built with `cargo build --release` and run with `--verbose` in a
  throwaway world, with the lab listserver. Every frame went through the
  decrypting proxy.
- Clients: real 2.05 rev 2 (`GNW03081`, gen 3 on the wire) and real 2.22
  (`GNW03014`, gen 5), each in its own Wine prefix. All of them were active
  (none paused), stood next to each other in one level, and had 3 hearts.
  Step d used two 2.22s.

## Results on PR #80

| | what was done | result |
|---|---|---|
| a | 2.22 swings at 2.05 | The 2.05 got `PLO_OTHERPLPROPS` (8) `20 22 2a 25 73776f7264` (the 2.22's id, then prop 10 `sword`). It sent back prop 2 power 5 with `hurt` (`22 25 2a 24 68757274 …`) and its hearts dropped to 2.5. **Pass.** |
| b | 2.05 swings at 2.22 | The 2.22 got `20 21 2a 25 73776f7264`, sent `22 25 2a 24 68757274 …`, and its hearts dropped to 2.5. **Pass.** |
| c | 2.05 hits the 2.22 until it dies | After 6 hits the 2.22 sent `PLI_CLAIMPKER` (14) `20 21` (killer id 1), then `dead` with prop 2 = 0, and showed its death screen. The server logged the kill claim, sent the killer alignment 0 (prop 32), and relayed the victim's `dead`. After logout the killer's account had `KILLS 1`. **Pass.** |
| d | two 2.22s swing at each other | Each got the other's id plus `*%sword`, hurt itself to 2.5 hearts, and showed "2.5" over the other. **Pass.** |

## Before: `main`

Both clients sent `*%sword` (prop 10) as they swung. The server dropped it
for the 2.05 in both directions, because seihai-core's 2.05 profile reads
prop 10 as the bow slot. Neither client was sent the other's swing, and
both stayed at 3 hearts.

## Where seihai still differs from beta4

Neither difference changed what either client drew.

- When it relays the victim's props, seihai puts prop 15 (X) after prop 17.
  The client sent `" % * $ hurt / ^ 1 …`, and seihai relays
  `" % * $ hurt 1 … / ^`. beta4 keeps the client's order, and also adds
  `n'@`.
- beta4 echoes the gani back to the victim as `PLO_PLAYERPROPS` (9)
  `* $ hurt 1 …`. seihai sends no 9 to the victim.

The kill (c) was not run on beta4, so there is nothing to compare it with.

## What this settles for #50

A real 2.x hit is not a hit message. The attacker's client sends its
`sword` gani (prop 10), and the server relays it to the others in the
level. The victim's client sees the gani next to it, hurts itself, and
sends its own hearts and `hurt` gani. When it dies, it names the killer
with `PLI_CLAIMPKER`. `PLI_HURTPLAYER` (26) did not appear in any of these
runs.
