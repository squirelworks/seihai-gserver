The first release of **seihai-gserver**, the Seihai project's Graal game server. It serves classic clients (1.33 to 2.31) and Seihai. It is written from scratch in Rust on `seihai-core` (MIT or Apache-2.0), with no GServer-v2 code.

## What it does

- **Login for every protocol generation:** gen 2 (1.21 to 1.39), gen 3 (1.41 to 2.17) and gen 5 (2.22 to 6.x).
  - A version allow-list, and flat-file accounts with PBKDF2 password hashes.
  - Secure login (`SEI00001`) for Seihai clients.
- **The world (stage 2):**
  - levels (`.nw`, `.graal`, `.zelda`), gmaps, links and warps, signs, and chests;
  - items on the floor, other players' props, and player flags;
  - the account keeps the player's level, position, hearts, rupees, arrows, bombs, glove, opened chests and flags.
- **Files:** `WANTFILE`/`UPDATEFILE` in each build's layout, large files in chunks, and `.gpak`/`.gpake` packages with their key sent first.
- **Client-side NPCs (stage 3):**
  - level NPCs and baddies, with one player per level leading the baddies;
  - bombs, shots, fire and blasts;
  - player hits and kill claims.
- **Server-side anti-cheat.** Speed, teleports, warps with no link and message floods are logged and put back. It only kicks where the admin turns that on.
- **The listserver link,** encrypted by default, with optional account checks through the listserver.
- **The NPC-server link** to seihai-npcserver v0.1.0. With `npcserver = auto`, the gserver starts the NPC-server itself.

## Tested with

- Loopback tests for login, the world, files, NPCs and both links.
- Real 1.39, 2.05 rev 2, 2.17.1, 2.22 and 2.31 clients under Wine on loopback only (`docs/lab`).

## Not in this release

- **Real clients' hits.** In the stage 3 lab run, the real clients did not take or send player hits. A player saved with 0 hearts logs back in with 0.
- **RC and NC endpoints for seihai-rc** (stage 4).
- **Server-side NPC scripts.** Only half of the NPC-server link is done; the NPC-server's messages back to the gserver are not handled yet (stage 5).
- **3.x to 6.x features** (stage 6).

## Download and run

Pick the archive for your system: Linux x86_64, Windows x86_64, macOS Intel (x86_64) or macOS Apple Silicon (aarch64).

```
seihai-gserver --world path/to/world
GRAAL_PW=... seihai-gserver account test1 --password-env GRAAL_PW --world path/to/world
```

The settings and their defaults are in `world/config/server.txt` in the source. On macOS, the first start may need right-click → Open, because the build is not signed.
